***Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)***

This helm has 3 charges. While wearing it, you can use an action and expend 1 charge to cast the *[teleport](https://grimoire.thebombzen.xyz/spells/teleport)* spell from it. The helm regains **1d3** expended charges daily at dawn.
