***Potion, rare***

When you drink this potion, you gain the “reduce” effect of the *[enlarge/reduce](https://grimoire.thebombzen.xyz/spells/enlarge-reduce)* spell for **1d4** hours (no concentration required). The red in the potion’s liquid continuously contracts to a tiny bead and then expands to color the clear liquid around it. Shaking the bottle fails to interrupt this process.
