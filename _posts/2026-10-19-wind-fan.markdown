***Wondrous item, uncommon***

While holding this fan, you can use an action to cast the *[gust of wind](https://grimoire.thebombzen.xyz/spells/gust-of-wind)* spell (save DC 13) from it. Once used, the fan shouldn’t be used again until the next dawn. Each time it is used again before then, it has a cumulative 20 percent chance of not working and tearing into useless, nonmagical tatters.
