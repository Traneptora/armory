***Wondrous item, rare***

If the stone is touching the ground, you can use an action to speak its command word and summon an earth elemental, as if you had cast the *[conjure elemental](https://grimoire.thebombzen.xyz/spells/conjure-elemental)* spell. The stone can’t be used this way again until the next dawn. The stone weighs 5 pounds.
