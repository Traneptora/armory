***Wondrous item, common***

The blue crystal of this earring is wrapped with delicate copper wire. The earring has 5 charges. While wearing it, you can use an action to expend 1 charge and cast the *[message](https://grimoire.thebombzen.xyz/spells/message)* spell. The earring regains **1d4 + 1** expended charges daily at dawn.
