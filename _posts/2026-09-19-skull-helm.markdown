***Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)***

While wearing this skull-shaped helm, you have resistance to cold, poison, and necrotic damage.

Additionally, while wearing the helm, you can cast *[Spirit of Death](https://grimoire.thebombzen.xyz/spells/spirit-of-death)* from it without requiring material components. Once you use the helm to cast the spell, the helm can’t cast this spell again until the next dawn.
