***Martial melee weapon (trident), uncommon (requires attunement)***

This trident is a magic weapon. It has 3 charges. While you carry it, you can use an action and expend 1 charge to cast *[dominate beast](https://grimoire.thebombzen.xyz/spells/dominate-beast)* (save DC 15) from it on a beast that has an innate swimming speed. The trident regains **1d3** expended charges daily at dawn.
