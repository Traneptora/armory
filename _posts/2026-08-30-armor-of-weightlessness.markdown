***Armor, uncommon (requires attunement)***

This armor has 5 charges. While you wear it, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells from the armor, targeting yourself: *[Jump](https://grimoire.thebombzen.xyz/spells/jump)* (1 charge) or *[Levitate](https://grimoire.thebombzen.xyz/spells/levitate)* (2 charges).

This armor regains **1d4 + 1** expended charges daily at dawn.
